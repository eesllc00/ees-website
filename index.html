Supply Scope

Defined around the requirement.

Product selection begins with the solicitation: dimensions, performance criteria, packaging, delivery terms, site access, and required documentation.

01 ↗

Containers & Enclosures

Standard and purpose-built units for storage, transport, workspace, and equipment protection.

  • 10-, 20-, and 40-foot dry freight containers
  • High-cube, refrigerated, insulated, and modified units
  • Specialty shipping cases and cylinder containers
02 ↗

Modular & Site Facilities

Temporary and permanent space configured around the stated site and operational requirement.

  • Mobile offices and modular buildings
  • In-plant rooms, shelters, and storage structures
  • Delivery, setup, ramps, stairs, and accessories when specified
03 ↗

Equipment & Material Handling

Commercial equipment furnished to the required capacity, configuration, and destination.

  • Generators, lifts, telehandlers, and compact equipment
  • Trailers, load banks, and jobsite equipment
  • Purchase or scheduled use when a solicitation requires it
04 ↗

Fabricated & Industrial Products

Specified products that require technical review, manufacturer confirmation, or custom fabrication.

  • Lockers, cabinets, racks, and secure storage systems
  • Metal components, shelters, and equipment housings
  • Specialized commercial and military-use products

Contract Execution

From specification to acceptance.

Supply work is managed against the complete requirement—not only the item description. Delivery terms, documentation, and site conditions are treated as part of the product.

01

Requirement Review

Dimensions, salient characteristics, clauses, packaging, destination, schedule, and submission requirements are reviewed together.

02

Product Confirmation

The proposed make, model, configuration, technical data, availability, and lead time are checked against the stated requirement.

03

Delivery Planning

Freight, appointments, site access, unloading, setup, and installation responsibilities are defined before award execution.

04

Contract Administration

Submittals, order status, modifications, delivery records, invoicing, and closeout are managed through completion.
